Tarhuala

I'm a business analyst. I have spent more time with made-up people than with most of the real ones I know, and I started young.

Tarhuala is a pen name. It means “a thread of words,” stitched from tar (Persian and Urdu for thread), hua (Mandarin for speech), and la (from Spanish and French), language as the material stories get made out of.

I write horror and survival because the human mind does its most interesting work under pressure. I would rather watch that happen to someone invented than to anyone real.

If I had one wish, it would be that the world outside is not as gray as it sometimes feels.

Tarhuala
